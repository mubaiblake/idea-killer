# Verdict: Pivot

The market may be real, but your current version is too close to "AI agency / AI operator". It will pull you into high-trust selling, heavy concierge delivery, and a know-how-deep vertical you don't yet have.

## Why the score was capped

| Founder constraint | Why it pulls the score down |
|---|---|
| No seller access | Validation needs 5+ real seller owners showing you weekly ops reports |
| No willingness to BD | The early version requires sustained relationship-building |
| 6-8 month runway | Concierge delivery + trust ramp is at minimum 12 months to first revenue |

## A more adapted version

Don't sell an "AI employee". Build a weekly decision brief seller bosses actually want to read:

- Selection opportunities
- Ad anomalies
- Inventory risks
- Competitor moves

Keep only the 3 items per week that change a decision. No dashboard, no chat UI, no agent. A PDF or email. Cheap to ship, cheap to iterate.

## The right next step

This is not a "build the product" step. It is a "test the access" step:

> Find 5 Amazon seller owners. Get them to show you last week's real ops report.

If none will → it's not a product problem, it's an access problem → Kill.
If 2-3 will → run idea-killer again with the reshape on top of the new evidence.

## What this report deliberately does not do

- It doesn't tell you to find a co-founder
- It doesn't tell you to spend 6 months "doing more research"
- It doesn't tell you the market is huge and you should be excited

The job here is to prevent the wrong sprint, not to make the idea prettier.
