# Verdict: Go

This direction matches you on every load-bearing dimension:

- **Channel**: indie devs are reachable on X / GitHub / Reddit (text-async, English audience)
- **Craft**: copy + UX critique + concrete fixes — your AI/writing skill stack maps directly
- **Delivery**: a single PDF / one-page report, no concierge support, no real-time
- **Trust**: low — recipients judge your output, not your relationships

## Why it's still capped slightly

A `Go` is not "free pass". The fit-score sits below 8 because:

- Long-term content cadence (1-2 posts/week) is required to compound demand
- You haven't yet validated a price point that doesn't sit at $0

## What `Go` actually means

`Go` does NOT mean "start building SaaS today".

It means: this idea earns the right to enter a deeper workflow. The recommended next steps:

1. Run `office-hours` to sharpen the wedge (which segment of indie devs? which page type?)
2. Run `startup-design` for full positioning + GTM
3. Validate paying-customer signal: book 5-10 free reviews, ask 3 to convert to paid at $50-150

## What evidence would convert this `Go` into a `Kill`

- 0 of 10 free reviewers respond
- All respondents say "thanks, I'll take it from here" without any of the recommended changes
- Indie dev content cadence becomes infeasible after 2 weeks of ship-in-public

## What evidence would lock it in

- 2 paying conversions out of the first 10 free reviews
- 1 piece of content reaches 50+ engagements, indicating channel reach
- A repeat customer asks for a second site review within 30 days
