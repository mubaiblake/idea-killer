# Verdict: Kill

The biggest problem with this idea is not whether AI can do it. It's whether you can sell it.

In legal services, purchasing trust comes from referrals, reputation, prior cases, and long-term relationships. You don't have a real entry point into this industry, and you're not willing to do sustained offline BD. Even if your demo looks great, you won't reach a paying customer.

## It re-skins an old pattern

You've been pulled toward similar directions before:

- A vertical industry that looks like it pays well
- AI clearly improves efficiency
- But you're not an industry insider
- Validation always stalls at "who will take a serious meeting with me"

This is not a problem that disappears by switching to a different vertical.

## What evidence would overturn this Kill

Exactly one piece of evidence:

> Within 7 days, you book 5 meetings with law-firm partners or client-acquisition leads, and 2 of them show you their real funnel.

If you can't, don't write product code.

## A more adapted reshape

Keep your strengths: AI, writing, information synthesis, solo delivery.

Drop high-trust selling:

- Landing-page diagnostics for indie developers
- Cold-start content audits for small-B founders
- Public-data anomaly reports for cross-border sellers

These can be sold via content and sample reports — no closed industry network required.
