## ⭐ The actual breakthrough: the meta-case

The most useful output wasn't any single Kill. It was what the skill said after killing 9 in a row:

> "Your 9 Kills are not because you didn't think of enough ideas.
> They're because the 'asymmetric advantages' I was assuming for you
> were halo assumptions, not real moats.
> Drop down to the actual competitive layer of each market —
> not the resume layer — and you're at zero in every one of them.
> This is profile mismatch, not idea-supply problem."

The skill stopped recommending a 10th idea and gave three meta-options instead:

1. Find a co-founder (you're not solving this solo)
2. Lower expectations (indie side-project, not venture-scale)
3. Embed for 6 months (join an early team, accumulate know-how + relationships)

That moment was the actual product. See [`case-study-halo-trap.md`](case-study-halo-trap.md) for the full transcript.

- #07 fitness, #09 cross-border picker, #10 cross-border operator

### ② Soft-value subscription + low willingness to pay

- #01 #02 #04 #05 #06

### ③ General AI will absorb this in 6-12 months

- #01 #02 #05 #06

### ④ Channel mismatched to founder profile

- Almost every ToC idea required content-IP / on-camera / cold-outreach — none of which the founder will sustain

## How to read a report

Each report includes:

- `verdict` + `score_pressure`
- 5 hard-condition gates
- 8-dimension score breakdown
- Most lethal assumption / most critical finding
- Falsification condition (what evidence would overturn the Kill)
- 1-2 reshapes (if you don't want to fully drop it)

The point isn't the score. It's the `cap_reason` — a single sentence naming which part of *you* killed the idea.
